# MIDI Workbench privacy policy

Effective September 21, 2026.

MIDI Workbench does not collect or store your files, mappings, or personal information in application databases or on an application server. Imported files are read locally in your browser; their contents are not uploaded.

Your project exists only in the current tab's memory. The app does not save it in cookies, local storage, session storage, or IndexedDB. Refreshing or closing the tab discards the workspace. Download a ZIP to keep a copy on your own device. A legacy autosave from earlier versions is removed when the app opens.

The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, accounts, or external database connections. Editing, importing, and exporting do not send project data over the network. Your browser still requests the application's static files from the host and may cache them.

When hosted on Vercel, the site uses Vercel's infrastructure to deliver and secure those files. Vercel records traffic and operational information, which can include IP addresses and request metadata, even when Web Analytics is not enabled. The application does not control hosting logs. Our no-storage statement concerns the application's handling of your files and mappings; it does not promise that the hosting provider stores no traffic information. See [Vercel Observability](https://vercel.com/docs/observability) and [Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice).

ZIP exports are generated in your browser and saved only when you choose to download them. You control those copies. Documentation and source links open other websites only when followed; their privacy policies apply there.
